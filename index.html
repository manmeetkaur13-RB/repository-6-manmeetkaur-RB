How this demo works

  • Choose an answer and use the question navigator to move around the test.
  • Answers are saved in memory while the exam is open.
  • Changing tabs or minimizing the window records a visibility event. Leaving fullscreen records an exit only if fullscreen was entered.
  • The experimental activity indicator is a simple illustrative score, not a trained AI model.
  • Submit your exam early or wait for the timer to expire.
